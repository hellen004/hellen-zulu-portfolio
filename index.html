About

I'm a Computer Science graduate of the University of Zambia and a software engineer by profession based in Lusaka. I like taking an application from a set of requirements through data modelling, API design and a working interface, and then putting it somewhere people can use it.

Most of my work is JavaScript: React on the front, Node.js and Express behind it, and PostgreSQL as the datastore I reach for first. I've also built with MongoDB and Firebase where they suited the product better.

In the past my work is split between building internal software and writing bids, analysing RFPs and tenders and turning their evaluation criteria into compliant submissions. That keeps me close to requirements, deadlines and documentation, which are the things that decide whether software gets used.
